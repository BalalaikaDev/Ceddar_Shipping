# M02 — Plot a course

Your first run: port A to port B. Given the **distance** (nautical miles) and
your **speed** (knots), report the ETA in hours.

## Your task
In `missions/m02_course.py`, make `eta_hours(distance, speed)` return
`distance / speed`.

## What you're learning
- Arithmetic: `+ - * /`.
- You can `return` a calculation directly: `return distance / speed`.
- time = distance ÷ speed.
